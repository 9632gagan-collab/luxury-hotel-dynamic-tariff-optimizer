Stage 2 Roadmap

The Luxury Hotel Tariff Optimizer optimizes room pricing in response to real-time occupancy. Below is the mathematical multiplier formula designed in Stage 1 architecture:

Occupancy Range Demand Tier Pricing Multiplier Deluxe Room (₹4,500 Base) Suite (₹8,500 Base) Presidential (₹18,000 Base)
< 40% Low Demand / Incentive 0.90x (-10% Discount) ₹4,050.00 ₹7,650.00 ₹16,200.00
40% - 70% Standard Nominal Demand 1.00x (Standard Rate) ₹4,500.00 ₹8,500.00 ₹18,000.00
70% - 90% High Demand Surge 1.25x (+25% Surge) ₹5,625.00 ₹10,625.00 ₹22,500.00
> 90% Peak / Sold-Out Surge 1.50x (+50% Surge) ₹6,750.00 ₹12,750.00 ₹27,000.00
Note for Academic Viva: The mathematical functions are encapsulated within TariffCalculationHelper.java. Full automated real-time price updates across booking engines will be executed in Stage 2.
